## 📖 About

Stronghold Finder triangulates End Portal room coordinates from two or more Eye of Ender throws, displaying the exact stronghold location on your in-game map.

---

## ✨ Features
<a name="features"></a>

- **Eye Triangulation** — throw two Eyes and get exact coordinates instantly.
- **Map Marker** — marks stronghold on the in-game minimap.
- **Multi-Stronghold** — shows all 128 strongholds in the ring pattern.
- **Ring Distance Display** — tells you which ring your stronghold is in.
- **Travel ETA** — estimates walking/running time to the target.
- **Nether Shortcut** — shows equivalent Nether coordinates for fast travel.

> 🔑 **100% free** — no key system, no linkvertise, no ads.
